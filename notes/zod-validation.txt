Yes, 
[^A-Za-z0-9] 
actually matches any character that is NOT a letter or a number.The ^ symbol at the very beginning of the square brackets [^ ... ] acts as a negation operator in regular expressions.

How It Works:
[A-Za-z0-9] matches any alphanumeric character.[^A-Za-z0-9] matches anything else, 
which effectively includes all symbols and punctuation (special characters) 

such as:!, @, #, $, %, ^, &, *, (, ), -, _, +, =, etc.It also includes spaces, tabs, and line breaks.
